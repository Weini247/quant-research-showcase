01 / POINT FORECASTS

No clear improvement.

Small advantages change with the horizon. All eight model-versus-no-change block intervals include zero. Inconclusive evidence is not equivalence.

02 / DISTRIBUTIONS

A qualified positive result.

Chronos-2 has lower descriptive weighted interval scores than static training quantiles. That limited comparator does not isolate pretraining benefit.
