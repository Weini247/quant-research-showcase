# Research scope and boundaries

The public empirical case study is the completed 2018–2019 FX development comparison. Its five benchmark variants, stored uncertainty and descriptive probabilistic diagnostics are reported without a claim of independent generalization.

The wider model benchmark remains incomplete; no model substitution is made. Future transfer evaluation requires a separate research instruction and cannot be used to retune the exposed development comparison. No new experiment follows from this publication.

Economic validation, workbench integration and adaptive research capability remain separate future questions. No paper/live operation, trading integration, profitability or delivered autonomous system is claimed. P1 remains not activated.
